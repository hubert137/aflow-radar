# Third-party components
No third-party dependency binaries are committed. Packages are resolved from their official PyPI distributions during build and retain their packaged notices.
Installed metadata checked for the pinned dependency set:
- pandas: BSD-3-Clause
- plotly / plotly.js: MIT (local JS copied from installed package)
- requests: Apache-2.0
- numpy: BSD-3-Clause AND 0BSD AND MIT AND Zlib AND CC0-1.0
- certifi: MPL-2.0
- charset-normalizer: MIT
- idna: BSD-3-Clause
- packaging: Apache-2.0 OR BSD-2-Clause
- python-dateutil: BSD-3-Clause
- pytz: MIT
- tenacity: Apache-2.0
- tzdata: Apache-2.0
- urllib3: MIT
Consult each exact distribution for full licenses/notices and bundled components. Python official container includes additional OS/Python components and their respective notices.
Upstream application attribution and the missing standalone upstream license file are documented in UPSTREAM.md. Eastmoney market data is not included or relicensed.
